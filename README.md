Cyber Clinic Day for Not for Profits - 7 October

Join us at La Trobe University’s Cyber Clinic Day, a practical and interactive event designed to help not for profit organisations better understand their cyber risks and strengthen their cyber security.

The day brings together NFPs, cybersecurity students, academics and industry professionals to share knowledge, discuss current threats and provide practical support.

12:00 pm to 1:00 pm | Networking Lunch
Join us for lunch and connect with other NFPs, industry professionals, La Trobe staff and students.

1:00 pm to 3:00 pm | NFP Cyber Clinic
The session will include:

    Cyber Threat Panel: Hear from experts about current and emerging threats affecting NFPs, including scams, phishing, data and privacy risks, and AI enabled threats.

    Cyber Health Check: Explore your organisation’s current cyber practices and identify areas where security could be strengthened.

    Drop in Cyber Support: Bring your questions and speak with cybersecurity students, academics and industry mentors for practical guidance.

    Tools and Resources: Discover useful Australian Government resources and industry good practices that you can continue using after the event.

No specialist cyber knowledge is required. The focus is on practical steps that NFPs can realistically take to reduce risk and build cyber resilience.

Wednesday 7 October 2026 | 12:00 pm to 3:00 pm
Digital Innovation Hub, La Trobe University, Bundoora

Free to attend. Lunch provided.
